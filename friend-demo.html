What the system does

PHC requirement tracking
Readiness, missing evidence, review and action states.
Digital evidence
Documents/photos can be organized against relevant requirements in an authenticated tenant.
Digital registers
18 operational compliance/admin registers are available in the current clinic pack.
Human-controlled exceptions
Ambiguous or specialty-specific issues go to review; the system does not invent clinical/regulatory decisions.
